This repository is for our IS117 Group Business project at NJIT. Our goal is to work together to research, plan, design, and develop a business website that meets the needs of our target audience.

Throughout this project, we will compare existing websites, explore different business approaches, make design decisions based on our research, and improve our work through teamwork and peer feedback.

## Research and Design Process

Our group will follow these steps:

1. **Research:** Compare three relevant websites and collect evidence.
2. **Positioning:** Compare two possible business approaches.
3. **Audience:** Develop two persona hypotheses and select our target audience.
4. **Design:** Choose our brand character, visual style, and two honest persuasion techniques.
5. **Features:** Connect research findings to proposed website features.
6. **Verification:** Create observable checks to evaluate our decisions.
7. **Peer Review:** Collect feedback, respond, and improve our documentation or website.

Each important research finding will include its original source, research date, proposed decision, and verification check.

We will clearly distinguish between confirmed evidence, assumptions, planned checks, and observed results.

## Peer Review Process

We will use GitHub Issues to request feedback and track improvements.

Our process:

1. Ask a reviewer to inspect our research and trace a finding from its original source to a proposed design decision.
2. Record the reviewer's feedback in a GitHub issue.
3. Decide whether to implement, investigate, defer, or decline the suggestion.
4. Explain our reasoning in the issue.
5. Make changes when needed and link the relevant commit or pull request.
6. Verify the changes and document the results.
7. Ask the reviewer to retest before closing a resolved issue.

Unfinished issues will remain open with a clear next step.
