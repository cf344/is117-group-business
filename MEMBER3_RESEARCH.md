# Member 3 — Beauty Salon Website Research

**Researcher:** Tasnim Fariha  
**Research date:** November 23, 2026  
**Website reviewed:** GlossGenius — salon booking software and example booking website features  
**Original sources:** https://glossgenius.com/customers/salon-software and https://glossgenius.com/custom-website-builder

## What I observed
GlossGenius describes salon booking sites with a service menu, optional portfolio images, customizable design, and a login-free booking flow. It also describes business-controlled appointment approval settings and cancellation/deposit policies. These are features presented by the vendor, not results from testing a specific salon's live booking form.

## Strengths
- The service menu and gallery can help customers understand available services and see examples before requesting an appointment.
- Login-free booking reduces the number of steps required for a first-time visitor.
- Explicit booking approval controls allow the business to distinguish a submitted request from a confirmed appointment.

## Weaknesses or limitations for our project
- The product advertises many features, including payments and policies, that would add complexity beyond our class project's scope.
- These pages describe the product, not a single salon's customer experience; they cannot prove that a particular salon's navigation or form works well.
- The vendor pages do not independently establish accessibility or performance of a real salon site. Those need separate testing.

## Design decisions informed by the research
1. Show our four service categories with short descriptions and visible sample prices before the appointment form.
2. Keep the appointment request flow short and do not require an account.
3. Clearly display **Request received — pending approval** on the confirmation page.
4. Avoid payments and automatic appointment guarantees, since our project only handles requests.

## Planned verification
- Can a new visitor find the services and prices without searching through multiple pages?
- Can someone submit a request without signing in?
- Does the confirmation accurately show service, date/time, request ID, and pending status?
- Can the flow be completed with keyboard navigation and on a narrow screen?

## Evidence and limitations
This is an original-source **feature review**, not hands-on usability testing of a live salon website. The group should confirm with the instructor whether a salon software provider's booking-site examples meet the requirement for reviewing an *existing beauty salon website*. If not, replace this source with a specific salon's public booking website and record direct observations. No tests were executed as part of this research.
