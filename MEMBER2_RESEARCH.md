# Member 2 — Beauty Salon Website Research

**Researcher:** Clara (Member 2)  
**Website:** European Wax Center  
**Original URL:** https://waxcenter.com/  
**Research date:** November 23, 2026

## Research Question

How does an existing beauty salon website make the appointment booking process easy for customers?

## My Observation

I explored the European Wax Center website to understand how customers book appointments online. I found the booking process easy to follow. The website helped me understand how a beauty business can make booking more convenient for customers.

## Strength

One strength I noticed was that the overall booking process felt simple and easy to understand. This is important because customers should be able to request appointments without getting confused.

## Limitation

I did not identify a specific problem during my review. However, I have not tested every feature, so I cannot say that the entire website works perfectly.

## How This Research Influences Our Website

For our beauty salon website, I want to create a simple appointment request form that customers can complete without unnecessary steps.

The form will allow customers to select a service, choose a preferred date and time, and enter their contact information.

I also want to make sure customers understand that submitting the form is only an appointment request and does not guarantee a confirmed booking.

## Planned Verification

To check whether our design works, I plan to:

- Ask a classmate to complete a demo appointment request.
- Check whether the form is easy to understand.
- Test whether required fields display helpful error messages.
- Verify that the selected service, date, and time appear correctly on the confirmation page.

**Testing status:** These checks are planned and have not been completed.
